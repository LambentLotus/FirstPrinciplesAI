# FirstPrinciplesAI

FirstPrinciplesAI is a Ruby toolkit designed for researchers, data scientists, and artists who approach complex AI challenges not as black-box classification problems, but through the lens of computational forensics and mathematical first principles.

This toolkit provides a modular, transparent foundation for applying universal scaling laws, topological analysis (fractal geometry), and interpretable visualization techniques to diverse datasets. It rejects heuristic-heavy, opaque model architectures in favor of reproducible, mathematically grounded pipelines.

## Core Philosophy
* **Explainability by Design:** Outputs must be verifiable and interpretable by a domain expert without needing reverse engineering.
* **First Principles:** Solutions are derived from fundamental mathematical laws (e.g., power laws, scaling invariance) rather than pre-trained heuristics.
* **Cross-Domain Agnostic:** The underlying mathematics of complexity applies equally to biological tissue, astrophysical phenomena, and synthetic media.

Use FirstPrinciplesAI as a reliable shell to rapidly deploy core analytical protocols in new challenges, allowing you to focus your cognitive energy on the unique data characteristics of the problem at hand.
